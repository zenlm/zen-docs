# Welcome to Zen LM

Zen LM is the open model family of Zoo Labs Foundation, a 501(c)(3) non-profit. It is chosen for two jobs:

- **Agentic coding that runs on your own machine** — long context, tool use and fast decoding.
- **Marketing work** — content, campaigns and brand voice, with the images and video behind them.

## Available now: Zen 6 and Zen 6 Flash

- **Zen 6** — 27B dense; reads text, images and video; 262,144 tokens native, 1,048,576 with YaRN. [Weights](https://huggingface.co/zenlm/zen6) · `zen6` on api.hanzo.ai
- **Zen 6 Flash** — the ternary build of Zen 6: 1.77 bits per weight, 5.95 GB; reads images; runs on an Apple Silicon laptop. [Weights](https://huggingface.co/zenlm/zen6-flash) · `zen6-flash` on api.hanzo.ai

Specifications and serving commands are on [docs.zenlm.org](https://docs.zenlm.org/docs/models/zen6/).

## Research preview: Zen 7

Zen 7 is the next open-weight generation. It has no weights yet and cannot be called. [Request access](https://hanzo.ai/research-access).

## Quick Start

```python
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer

model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained("zenlm/zen-nano")
tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained("zenlm/zen-nano")

inputs = tokenizer("Hello, I am", return_tensors="pt")
outputs = model.generate(**inputs, max_length=100)
print(tokenizer.decode(outputs[0]))
```
